# 熊妖侧身行走母图 prompt（请用户出图）

用途：给 4–6 级熊妖（bear-humanoid）重做行走动画用的参考图。请用现有熊妖立绘 `design/xianxia-bear-humanoid-2026-11-02/original.png` 作为参考图上传，保持同一角色。

**Prompt：**
Same brown bear warrior as the reference image, full body, standing upright on two hind legs in a relaxed walking-ready stance, shown in a strict side profile view facing LEFT: head, muzzle, chest and both feet pointing to the left edge of the image, only the right side of the body visible, the far arm and far leg mostly hidden behind the body. Keep the same bear head, rounded ears, thick brown fur, shoulder armor, bracers, round belt buckle and leather kilt, same colors and proportions, exactly two arms and two legs, claws visible. Feet flat on the ground, weight evenly on both legs. Pure flat green background #00FF00, no ground, no shadow, no props, no text. The whole body fits inside the frame with about 10% margin, subject centered, crisp detailed painterly game art matching the reference style.

**要求：**
- 1024×1024 或更大，绿底，主体完整、四周留白。
- 必须是严格侧身朝左；三分之四侧也可以，但脸和胸口不能朝向镜头。
- 出图后发给我，我来检查验收、补洞、上传，再用 Scenario 生成行走。

**注意：** 侧身行走接入后，熊妖站着不动时显示的仍是现在偏正面的立绘，从走到停会有一次朝向切换。如果希望站立时也是侧身，需要再决定是否把立绘一起换掉。
