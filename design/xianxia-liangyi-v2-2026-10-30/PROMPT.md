# 两仪剑阵加强版制作意图

本轮用现有素材做分层和动作编排，不提交付费模型任务。

**中文：** 保持两仪剑阵的固定圆形边界和阴阳双眼。增强青蓝色阵纹与白色剑锋，以低透明暗蓝底色拉开地面对比，不能变成遮挡敌人的实心圆盘。真正的剑雨从角色与怪物上方快速坠下，不再只是地面上淡淡滑动的小剑。每次脉冲前短暂显剑，随后加速下落；真实命中瞬间有清晰的白锋、局部触地闪光和向外飞散的细碎剑芒，停锋片刻再迅速消隐。普通轮转由单眼发动；10级第七次脉冲双眼同亮，落剑更强，向心收阵。残留阵纹缓慢消散。

所有落剑在同一拍抵达，不通过增加打击次数制造热闹。亮点反馈对应真实受击目标。保持地面和敌人可读，不加全屏白闪、不反复挥剑、不移动阵心、不改变选点或伤害。

**English:** Strengthen the existing Liangyi formation using the preserved art. Keep the fixed circular ground boundary and two yin-yang sword seals. Use more saturated cyan-blue tracery, bright white blade cores, and a restrained dark-blue foundation to separate the pattern from the stone floor. Keep enemies readable.

Render the sword rain on an aerial layer. Materialize clear, substantial sword silhouettes shortly before each real pulse, accelerate them downward, and land them together on the actual impact beat. Hold the bright blade edge briefly, add compact ground sparks and a fast dissipating flash, then let the residual formation fade gently. Only the rank-ten seventh pulse activates both seals together. Add localized impact highlights only at enemies that actually received damage. Do not change hit counts, targeting, field position, simulation speed, or character animation.
