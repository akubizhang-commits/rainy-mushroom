# 概念出图提示词

每个方向独立调用，不能将四个角色混在同一张动作板。生成前将以下公共要求与对应方向合并为一个提示词。

## 公共要求

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create an original, professionally art-directed character concept sheet for a hand-painted 2D side-scrolling adventure set in a miniature forest after rain. The character should feel alert, nimble, endearing, and quietly cool. Strong, elegant silhouette and expressive face, appealing shape design, coherent anatomy with deliberately stylized proportions. Beautiful restrained gouache-like shading, tactile cloth, confident drawn contours; clean major color shapes readable at small gameplay scale. Avoid generic mobile-game plastic rendering, mannequin limbs, crude vector geometry, robot helmets, black circular face screens, visual clutter, oversized boots, sagging posture, rear bags, butt protrusions, weapons and franchise likenesses.
Composition: precisely four full-body depictions of ONE identical character in a spacious 2 by 2 arrangement on a uniform warm light grey background. Upper left: relaxed but alert three-quarter standing pose. Upper right: lively side-view light running pose facing right. Lower left: anatomically coherent hands-and-knees crawl facing right, palms and knees support the body, shoulders raised, chest and abdomen clearly off the ground, face looking forward. Lower right: a light, compact airborne jump facing right, with naturally bent knees. Consistent facial features, outfit, colors and proportions in all four depictions. Each body fully inside its own quadrant with ample margin; no overlaps or cropped fingers, feet, hats. No labels, no text, no decorative frames, no landscape, no UI. This is a concept board, not an animation sequence or game atlas. Clothing must adapt plausibly to poses. Do not paste the standing torso onto crawling limbs.

## A · 林间少年

Subject: a tiny youthful human woodland scout, androgynous, approximately 3.3 heads tall. Fully visible warm skin face with an expressive, curious but determined gaze; softly tousled chestnut short hair, uncovered forehead, no hat or hood. Moss-green sleeveless short field vest over an ivory rolled-sleeve shirt; charcoal shorts, soft wrapped ankle shoes. Small amber neckerchief and one seed-shaped waist fastening. Slim rounded torso, clear elbows, knees and hands; lightweight practical silhouette. Youthful and capable, not infantile. No backpack, cape, face mask or dangling accessories. The face and natural hair silhouette are the primary emotional anchors.

## B · 叶帽游侠

Subject: an original miniature forest ranger, androgynous, approximately 3.5 heads tall, with a soft asymmetrical folded-leaf pointed hat leaning back and to one side. Entire warm face visible, short dark hair, focused expressive eyes and a hint of self-assurance. Deep pine-green fitted short tunic, a single short leaf shoulder cape ending above the waist, pale grey-beige slim trousers, orange-red fabric belt, flexible low ankle shoes. Elegant diagonal silhouette, springy athletic posture, subtle leaf construction, no hard armor. Hat must leave the eyes clear while crawling. No weapon, long cape, backpack, tail or exaggerated elf ears. Cool through gesture, tailoring and expression rather than equipment.

## C · 雨披旅人

Subject: an original tiny rain-trail traveler, androgynous, approximately 3 heads tall. Soft low-crowned butter-yellow rain hat, small brim, expressive visible human face with warm cheeks and a few dark hair tufts. Cropped butter-yellow rain poncho with muted teal lining, side openings expose articulated forearms and hands, one small wooden clasp. Navy shorts, visible calves and light low waterproof shoes. Bright, warm, resilient and adventurous; supple matte fabric, not thick molded rubber. Poncho ends above the hips and folds against the back while crawling, hands and knees remain clearly visible, chest raised. No hood around the face, helmet, long coat, bag or oversized boots.

## D · 萤光森灵

Subject: an original tiny humanoid woodland spirit, approximately 2.7 heads tall. Warm ivory face with expressive dark eyes and subtle brows, short sculpted leaf-like hair fringe, a gentle but attentive expression. Slender rounded torso, clearly articulated little human-like arms, hands, knees and feet. Blue-grey cropped jacket, a small soft shoulder mantle and a single dim amber glowing chest bead, dark flexible glove-like shoes. Restrained organic fantasy, poetic and confident. Minimal glow confined to the bead, face remains matte and readable. No black face void, antennae, wings, tail, snout, robot joints or backpack. Must remain recognizably a bipedal adventurer even in the hands-and-knees crawling pose, never a quadruped creature.
