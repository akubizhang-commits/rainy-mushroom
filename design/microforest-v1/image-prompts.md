## 共同约束

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: art direction concept for an original 2D side-scrolling miniature forest puzzle game.
Style: refined natural storybook illustration, subtle painted texture, restrained edge detail, organic asymmetry, wet foliage, quiet atmospheric depth. Clear playable middle ground; softer distant background. Side-on gameplay composition, not isometric, not a 3D screenshot. No UI, logos, watermarks, numbers or text. Do not imitate any named artist or game. Keep warm russet or ochre child explorer readable against cool forest greens. The character is ant-sized relative to plants, not illegibly small on screen. These are concept references, not ready-to-ship sprite atlases.

## 01 环境：露珠与叶桥

A tiny adventurer stands at the edge of a rain puddle in a forest floor world. A climbable grass stem rises to a broad leaf holding a single heavy dew drop. Beside it a separate long leaf can bend toward the opposite mossy bank. All essential puzzle objects visible in one readable side-view frame. Use the selected V2 explorer outfit with a visible face and close-fitting satchel. Cool teal background trunks, sage foliage, warm earth cross section, translucent dew with sparse specular highlights. No dense foreground blocking the traveler or platform edges. Reference: environment-dew.svg for composition and palette.

## 02 环境：风与绒毛

The same traveler suspended beneath a delicate dandelion seed tuft, crossing from a lower bank toward a raised mossy platform. A curled ochre dead leaf partly covers a wind opening. A few pollen motes communicate a rising air current without giant magical arrows. Bright backlit hairs, soft warm light in a cool green forest. Side-on gameplay layout, clear altitude difference and landing edge. Reference: environment-wind.svg.

## 03 环境：树根与甲虫

The same micro forest palette in a sheltered root corridor. Two unmistakable root hollows are safe hiding places, framed by textured bark lips. An original ground beetle patrols between them: segmented head, thorax, grooved blue-green wing cases, six articulated legs, expressive directional antennae, no human face. Warm light beyond a root arch marks the exit. The traveler is partially occluded inside the nearer hollow. Reference: environment-roots.svg.

## 04 主角（V2）

Two original tiny adventurers who feel quietly cool and a little cute, with rounded visible faces, compact proportions, confident relaxed poses and practical outdoor gear. Lan: tousled blue-black hair, asymmetrical short mist-blue shoulder cape, warm gold neck scarf, close-fitting satchel, gloves and sturdy ankle boots. Shuo: high auburn ponytail, compact goggles resting on the forehead without obscuring the eyes, terracotta cropped outdoor jacket, sage neck scarf, satchel and gloves. Distinct silhouettes, not palette swaps. No weapons, bulky armor, grim scowls, fashion heels or face-covering hoods. Show standing, walking, climbing and dandelion-gliding poses at consistent physical scale. Readable in 32–40 pixel silhouette studies. Equal abilities for this design phase; no class statistics or skill icons. Reference: character.svg, character-lan.svg, character-shuo.svg.

## 05 动物

Original micro-forest wildlife concept sheet on warm neutral paper. Primary subject: readable side-view ground beetle with six legs, differentiated head/thorax/wing cases, faint grooves, blue-green shell and warm gray joints. Secondary environmental subjects: moss-toned snail with spiral shell and soft body folds; slender warm-brown ant. Maintain consistent natural storybook rendering, restrained highlights and no anthropomorphic clothes. These are artistic designs, not taxonomic plates. Reference: wildlife.svg.

## 06 道具

Prop design sheet showing a substantial translucent dew drop on a leaf, a bendable leaf bridge in raised and lowered states, a delicate folded and open dandelion tuft, a curled dead leaf covering a wind opening, and a rough root hollow with separately readable foreground lip. Consistent light direction and natural materials. Make state differences readable through silhouette. Neutral background; no labels embedded in the generated image. Reference: props.svg.


## Decorative plant sheet update
Create twelve original non-interactive forest background forms: pendant bell flowers, five-petal woodland flowers, branching umbels, narrow grass tufts, pinnate ferns, cushion moss, pale-barked broadleaf trees, tiered conifers, spreading buttress roots, capped mushroom clusters, wood-growing shelf fungi and branching coral fungi. Reference plants.svg. Distinct silhouettes; restrained grey-green, mauve, grey-white and grey-brown. Group by habitat with negative space. Reserve luminous yellow-green, amber and clear cyan for interactive props. Never fill the forest with one repeated generic leaf. Artistic botanical concepts, not species identification plates.
